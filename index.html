How to set up a Glow Station
  1. Plug your phone in and prop it on a stand where you spend time.
  2. Go to Settings, then Display & Brightness, then Auto-Lock, and choose Never. Switch it back when you're done.
  3. For extra staying power, go to Settings, then Accessibility, then Guided Access, and turn it on.
  4. Open Glow Meter, tap Glow Station mode, then triple-click the side button and tap Start. Your phone stays on Glow Meter until you triple-click again.
